# Client Communication Masterclass Prep

## Big Idea: "Communication IS Sales"

**Thesis:** Every message you send is either building trust and positioning you as the strategic partner, or it's commoditizing you as an order-taker. Most editors fail before they ever start editing because they communicate like vendors, not partners.

---

## Framework 1: Price Last Checklist

### The Problem
Editors say their price too early in the conversation when there are so many more logistics still to figure out. This makes it feel like you're just trying to sell. People want to feel like special snowflakes—when they spend their money, they want something tailored specifically for them, not just something you do for other people.

### Things to Know Before Dropping the Price

✅ **Vision:**
- How many videos per month?
- What style are they going for?
- Any references they like?

✅ **Pain Points:**
- Are they working with an editor now?
- What's stopping them from hitting their goal?
- What's frustrating about their current process?

✅ **The Bridge:**
- How do you specifically solve their pain?
- What makes you different from their current situation?

### Action Sequence

**1. Connect Personally**
Move to WhatsApp/Telegram (less noise, no ghosting). Earn it first by providing value or doing initial research.

**2. Do the Research**
Look at their channel. Understand current situation, video formats, upload frequency, style. Make educated guesses on logistics instead of asking basic questions.

**3. Ask About Their Vision**
- Volume per month?
- Style they want?
- References they like?

**4. Spot the Pain**
- Are they working with an editor now?
- What's stopping them from hitting their goal?
- What's frustrating about their current process?

**5. Bridge the Gap**
Acknowledge their pain. Show how you specifically solve it:
- **Revisions?** → "I prioritize learning your style so revisions disappear"
- **Deadlines?** → "3-4 day turnaround, and I connect my rate to that reliability"
- **Quality?** → Send examples + reassure

**6. Drop the Price**
Only now. Connect price to solving their pain (e.g., "$500/video so I can prioritize and deliver in 3-4 days").

## Framework 2: Client-First Communication

### Universal Principles for Every Message

**Every message should:**
- Lead with client benefit
- Have one clear idea
- Stay human (vulnerable, direct, no BS)
- Ask one question at a time (don't overwhelm)

**For longer messages:**
- Pick one big idea for the message
- Cut anything that doesn't support it

### Good Communication Principles

- ❌ Never lie
- ❌ Never ghost
- ✅ Always confirm you're on it + give a deadline
- ✅ Position the client as the important person
- ✅ Be vulnerable and human in your messages

---

## Key Positioning Points

**Remember:**
- You're not selling editing. You're selling the transformation from their current shitty content situation to their dream content situation.
- The price isn't about "how much does editing cost"—it's about "what's it worth to close this gap?"
- Different formats = different pricing, so get clarity first.

**Tagline for students:**
*"If you say your price before you understand their problem, you're just another freelancer on Upwork."*
