## The Offer Stack (Mar 2026)

### FREE: Creative Director Shift™ (Skool)

**What:** Free 15-lesson course on Skool. The primary lead magnet and entry point into FTE.

**Structure:**

**Start Here:**
- 0.1 Why is this free?
- 0.2 What keeps you stuck (3 things)
- 0.3 The mindset foundation

**Pillar 1: Viewer-First Editing™**
- 1.1 What is good editing
- 1.2 What, not How™
- 1.3 Less is more
- 1.4 How to edit for the viewer

**Pillar 2: Quality-First Clients™**
- 2.1 Start before you're ready
- 2.2 The Price Last™ closing framework
- 2.3 Why you don't need more clients
- 2.4 How I charge $2.5K/video (weekly)

**Pillar 3: Freedom-First Workflow™**
- 3.1 The fear that makes you slow
- 3.2 Make $5K/month in 4 hours
- 3.3 Get paid for decisions, not time
- 3.4 Why you're not productive & unmotivated

**Next Steps:**
- How to join the paid 1-1 program

**Purpose:** Build trust, align people with the Creative Director vision, filter for fit. If they watch it in full, they're aligned with the FTE worldview.

**The us-vs-them filter:** Either you're on board with the Creative Director vision (4-2-1 routine, $5K/month with 1-2 premium clients) or you're a Grinder (agency model, 50 clients, $20K/month). If the second, FTE isn't for you.

**URL:** https://www.skool.com/full-time-editors

---

### $97: $1K in 30

**What:** 30-day email sequence with daily actions. Every day has a specific task plus a useful resource — Looms, templates, inspiration, reference points. Includes a Notion dashboard overviewing all 30 days.

**Who:** Editors below $1K/month. Unqualified for the main program.

**Promise:** Get to consistent $1K/month by building your brand on Twitter.

**Journey:** Unqualified leads from the "COCONUT" DM flow get directed here.

---

### $24.99: Paid Masterclass

**What:** Monthly live masterclass (on a Wednesday around the 15th). Recorded and available after. Free for FTE members. Public can join for $24.99.

**Purpose:** Micro offer that builds trust and demonstrates the depth of FTE coaching. Entry point into the Insight Program or full program.

**Cadence:** Sales emails drive to this around the 10th-15th of each month.

---

### $249: Insight Program (2 weeks)

**What:** A sneak peek into Full-Time Editor. Same deliverables as the full program, but for 2 weeks. The trust-builder for qualified leads who aren't ready for the full commitment.

**Who:** Qualified editors ($1K+/month) who want to experience FTE before committing to 6 months.

**Sales page:** Micro Offer Doc (separate from the Core Offer Doc).

**Cadence:** Sales emails drive to the Micro Offer Doc around the 15th-25th of each month.

**Journey:** Artem follow-up → Micro Offer Doc → Insight Program → upsell to Full-Time Editor.

---

### $3,000: Full-Time Editor (6 months)

**What:** 1:1 mentorship + community. The core program. Everything FTE offers — personalized editing reviews, weekly masterclasses, community, direct access to Artem.

**Pricing:**
* $3,000 PIF (pay in full)
* $1,750 x 2 payments
* $650 x 6 payments

**Who:** Qualified editors ($1K+/month consistently, 1-2 existing clients, ready for transformation).

**Prerequisite:** Must have completed the Creative Director Shift free course (or be aligned with the vision).

**Sales page:** Core Offer Doc (GHL tagged).

**Journey:** "COCONUT" DM → Qualify → Core Offer Doc → Buy / Don't buy → Artem follow-up → Book a call → FTE.

---

### Extension (existing members)

**Pricing:**
* $399/month
* $1,997/6 months
* $3,497/12 months

**Who:** Current FTE members who want to continue after their initial 6 months.
