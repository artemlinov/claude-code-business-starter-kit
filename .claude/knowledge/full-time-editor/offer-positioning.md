## Pain Points (Quick Reference)

* Feeling like a failure
* Pressure from parents/society ("what are you doing with your life?")
* Can't travel, stuck at parents' house
* Feeling stuck with no clear path forward
* Underpaid and overworked
* Not sure editing can be a real long-term career
* Clients treat them like a commodity
* Can recreate the animations but don't know what else is missing
* Confused about the gap between their skills and their income

*For detailed pain language with quotes, see `voice-of-customer.md`.*
