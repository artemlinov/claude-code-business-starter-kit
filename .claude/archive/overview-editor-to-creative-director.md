# Video Overview: Why Editors Never Make More Than $2K/Month

## Core Premise
Editors who stay "editors" cap out. The ones who break through become creative directors.

---

## Potential Titles:

- Why I Quit Editing
- I Quit Video Editing (to make $10k/month)
- Don't be a video editor.

## Key Topics to Cover

### The Problem
- Most editors are button-pushers, not creative thinkers
- They edit mechanically without considering the viewer's perspective
- Race to the bottom pricing because they're commoditized
- No vision = replaceable

### The Shift: Editor → Creative Director
- Creative direction = thinking from the viewer's perspective
- "Is this actually interesting? Should I remove this? Why is it this way?"
- Storytelling IS creative direction (same skill, reframed)
- It's not about better technical skills, it's about understanding the client

### Proof: The Kirill Story
- Hired Kirill to edit the vlog
- Instructions weren't detailed enough
- He doesn't have the creative vision to fill gaps
- Technical skill was there, creative direction wasn't

### Proof: The Starter Story Story
- Pat reached out for thumbnail help after months of no contact
- Not because you're the best designer
- Because you understand their channel, content, and audience deeply
- Depth with one client opens doors beyond your job title

### The Mechanism: Depth Over Volume
- Niche down to the CLIENT, not the niche
- Every revision is a free lesson in how your client thinks
- You're building a "context window" — all your experience compounds
- Volume resets understanding, leverage compounds it

### Frameworks to Reference
- Twist & Stick (especially Stick phase)
- The Cheat Code (revisions = learning)
- Storytelling First
- Sparkle Syndrome (the enemy — effects for ego, not viewer)

### The Outcome
- You become irreplaceable
- Clients reach out for work beyond your job title
- You can raise prices because you're not a commodity
- Creative directors don't compete on price
