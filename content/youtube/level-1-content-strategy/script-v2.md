## ENGAGEMENT TOOLKIT — Quick Reference

These are Kallaway-style tricks Alex can drop anywhere he feels the energy dipping:

| Trick | What it does | Example phrase |
|-------|-------------|----------------|
| **"But" pivot** | Creates conflict/tension after a setup | "Most brands try to start at Level 3. But that's exactly why they stall out." |
| **"Therefore" resolution** | Closes a loop, delivers payoff | "And therefore, the only thing that matters is how good the idea is executed." |
| **Short reset** | Snaps attention back after dense info | "That's it." / "Zero budget." / "That's the whole formula." |
| **Rhetorical bridge** | Re-engages before a new section | "So what does that actually look like?" / "So how do you do this?" |
| **Payoff preview** | Keeps them watching through current section | "And the next format is the one that surprises most brands." |
| **Contrast flip** | Breaks a belief, creates curiosity | "It's not about expensive gear. It's about being intentional with how you capture the moment." |
| **The callback** | Ties back to hook, creates cohesion | "Remember — phone and a brain. That's all you need for this one." |
