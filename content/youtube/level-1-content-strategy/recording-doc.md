## FORMAT 1: ONE-SHOT VIDEOS

- One clip, one moment, one idea
- Works at every stage of the funnel

**Level 1 — Lo-fi: single scene + text overlay**
- Text hook on screen + visual that brings it to life
- Example: Heart and Soil

> "But here's where the execution starts to separate."

**Level 2 — Hi-fi: single scene + text overlay with action**
- Layer in constant motion that works with the text
- Example: tomc_fit

**Level 3 — Vignettes**
- Short moments that transport the viewer into your world
- Over-optimize one thing: the angle, the aesthetic, or the action
- Example: another_adventure_gram

> "The ceiling is high, but the floor is free."

## FORMAT 2: TEN-SHOT VIDEOS

- Quick, snappy story through a mix of fast shots
- The pacing does the heavy lifting

**Level 1 — Text overlay**
- Text hook opens a curiosity gap, visuals close it
- Example: FUNBOY

> "The text overlay version is great to start with. But if you want to take this up a notch — there's a Level 2."

**Level 2 — Narration**
- Write a script, match each line to a specific shot
- Example: BlankedStudios

> "One-shots capture a moment. Ten-shots tell a story. But this third format... this is the one most brands overlook."

## FORMAT 3: CAROUSELS

> "There are three reasons brands should leverage carousels."

1. Best format for your existing followers
2. Great for re-packaging content that already worked
3. Can go nuclear — carousels get pushed into the Reels algorithm

> "Let me show you what this actually looks like."

- **Weekly vlog recap** — Example: Puresport
- **Campaign production BTS** — Example: Fern
- **Category entertainment or education** — Example: Graffeocoffee
- **ASMR** — Example: Fern
- **The Journey** — Example: Nude Project

> "Zero video skill required. Just photos, intention, and a story to tell."

> "Those first three formats are about capturing attention. But this last format is about building trust."

## FORMAT 4: HOW TO / WHY WE DO IT

- Explain how or why you do something
- Build credibility around what makes you different
- Collect your top comments, FAQ, competitor comments
- Answer them through the lens of your brand
- Example: Brownie God — "Why bakeries don't use chocolate bars"

> "This format works because it answers the questions people are already asking — but through the lens of your brand."
