# Video Overview: The AI Native Editor

## Core Premise
AI won't replace editors. It will widen the gap between button-pushers and creative thinkers.

---

## Key Topics to Cover

### The Fear (Address Head-On)
- Editors scared AI will replace them
- That fear is valid... for the wrong editors
- If you're just pressing buttons, you're cooked

### The Reality: AI Widens the Gap
- Top 1% editors think like creative directors, not editors
- AI automates repetitive/boring tasks
- More time for creative decisions
- But AI without good creative direction = slop

### The Key Insight
- AI needs YOUR creative thinking as input
- That's the skill: giving creative direction to AI
- Same way a client needs your creative direction
- You're the brain, AI is the tool

### How You Actually Use AI (Real Examples)

**1. Client DNA Builder**
- Create custom brand guidelines for clients
- Feed AI the context about client's vibe and style
- Then ask: what sound effect style? What search terms? What colors?

**2. Creative Decision Support**
- Brain dump your thoughts into AI
- "This music doesn't fit because it's too unstructured and noisy"
- "I need different colors but it clashes with brand guidelines"
- AI proposes solutions you wouldn't think of

**3. The Opacity Story**
- Tried bunch of different colors, nothing worked
- AI suggested: use opacity instead, keep everything red
- Also suggested circles instead of squares
- Result looked way better

**4. Research with Perplexity**
- Use REAL things the client talks about, don't make stuff up
- Most editors skip research or bug the client for resources
- Perplexity finds Instagram reels, YouTube videos, context
- Takes 2 minutes instead of hours

### The Bottom Line
- AI is an amplifier, not a replacement
- It amplifies creative thinking (if you have it)
- It exposes lack of creative thinking (if you don't)
- Reach top 1% ASAP or get left behind
